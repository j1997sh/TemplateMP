24 August 2026

Joe presses for more GP appointments in Bloggs Town

Joe has met local healthcare leaders to discuss access to appointments and the pressure facing practices across the constituency.

Residents have repeatedly raised difficulties getting timely appointments. Joe used the meeting to press for practical steps that could improve access locally and make better use of existing capacity.

He will continue raising the issue with local NHS leaders and ministers, and has asked residents to share their experiences through his local survey.
